Gandhi Gram · Vision 2030

One panchayat, one living model of Gram Swaraj.

A self-reliant, non-violent, fair and harmonious village, built with the people of Elappully rather than for them, and documented so other villages can follow.

Our approach

Participatory Action Research.

Instead of surveying a village from outside, the Ashram works with its people to find the root causes of their problems and to design the solutions together. Each idea is tested on a small scale, reviewed with the community, and grown only when it works.

  • Listen first: sample surveys, focus-group discussions and interviews with domain experts
  • Gram Swaraj Action Teams: ward-level committees of farmers, artisans, heritage craftspeople, SC representatives and educators
  • Pilot, review, scale: small pilots, community feedback, then systematic growth
  1. Phase 1: priority themes.
    Farming, handloom & khadi, Ramassery Idli, Antyodaya and Nai Talim education.
  2. Phase 2: every household.
    All 12,000 households in Elappully: income, health, sanitation, women's empowerment and peaceful co-existence.
  3. Then: a learning centre.
    Elappully as a Gandhian model for panchayats across Palakkad, Kerala, India and beyond.
The projects

Connected, not separate.

Water supports farming. Farming supports livelihoods. Heritage creates income. Health and Antyodaya protect dignity. Young people document, serve and lead.

Project 01

Young Gandhi Fellowship

A one-year leadership and service programme that brings college students from across Palakkad district into the work of Gandhi Gram, as researchers, volunteers, storytellers and coordinators.

Fellowship details
Project 02 · Krishi Swaraj

Sustainable Farming

Nearly 2,000 farming families and about 8,000 farm-labour families in Elappully face falling incomes. The project brings sustainable and eco-friendly farming, crop diversification and cost-cutting methods, with direct market access through Farmer Producer Organisations.

  • Soil health, scientific farming and expert crop protection
  • Bulk purchasing, shared machinery and trained labour support
  • Value addition and premium market access
  • Pilot model: 10 Krishi Koottams with 200 farmers
Project 03 · Heritage enterprise

Ramassery Idli

The Ramassery Idli is a two-century-old culinary heritage, and today only nine families still make it for a living. The project aims to turn that heritage into a dignified, higher-income enterprise without compromising authenticity.

  • Hygienic processing and longer shelf life
  • Packaging, branding and distribution, with the Geographical Indication (GI) tag
  • Heritage food tourism in Ramassery
Project 04 · Village economy

Thenari Weavers & Khadi Artisans

Nearly 250 traditional handloom and khadi artisan families face economic distress. The project brings modern design, technology, branding and e-commerce to the craft, and strengthens the cooperatives that keep it alive.

Project 05

Holistic Health & Wellness

Healthy-lifestyle education, mental wellness and meditation, elderly care and active ageing, and community health awareness for every ward.

Project 06 · Antyodaya

Social inclusion & dignity for SC communities

Antyodaya means uplifting the last person first. The project looks honestly at decades of development gaps and works for social equality, land security, sustainable livelihoods and self-respect for Scheduled Caste households in around 70 settlements, about 2,500 families.

  • Education, livelihood and empowerment
  • Youth and women's leadership
Project 07

Gram Swaraj: local democracy that works

Gram Swaraj is not only government. It means trained citizens, strong committees and regular review. The project covers all wards of Elappully Grama Panchayat.

  • Training for Ayalkoottams, Ayal Sabhas, ward committees and Gram Sabhas
  • Ward-level development action plans
  • Monthly review, social accountability and transparent follow-up

Train → Empower → Plan → Implement → Review → Improve

Proposed

Water-Abundant Village

Map and document existing water sources, clean and restore ponds and channels, recharge groundwater, harvest rainwater, and write a village water-management plan, building on the Rural Immersion Programme's water work with LEAD College and Unnat Bharat Abhiyan in October 2026.

Phase 1 theme · Nai Talim

Value-based education

Community Learning Centres, mentoring and holistic skill development for first-generation learners and children from families with little money.

What success looks like

A village others come to learn from.

Elappully becomes a Gandhian Model Learning Centre for panchayats across Palakkad, Kerala, other Indian states and international visitors, with outcomes documented for training, policy and practice.

See the planned institute
  • Stronger local participation and trained ward-level leadership
  • Better water conservation and sustainable farming
  • More income from heritage products and livelihoods
  • Health awareness, elderly support and mental wellness
  • Greater inclusion and dignity for disadvantaged families
  • A replicable village model others can study
