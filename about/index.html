About the Ashram

A memorial that serves the living.

Gandhi Ashram, Ramassery remembers the people of this village who answered Gandhi's call, and carries their work forward in the same village today.

Our story

Built on the model of Sevagram.

Gandhi Ashram stands in Ramassery, in Elappully Grama Panchayat of Palakkad district, a village better known across Kerala for its idlis than for its place in the freedom struggle. The Ashram was established on the model of Sevagram Ashram in Wardha, where Gandhi lived and worked from 1936.

It was founded in 2021, a year that brought together the Silver Jubilee of Sarvodaya Kendram and the 75th anniversary of India's Independence. It is both a memorial and a workplace: a centre for Gandhian thought and constructive social action on today's challenges of rural livelihoods, local governance, social justice and sustainability.

Its aim is concrete: to make Elappully a model Gram Swaraj Panchayat, rooted in truth, non-violence and the welfare of all.

History

Milestones of freedom and community.

  1. 1930

    The Dandi March

    Ramassery Vadavatte Thappan Nair joins Mahatma Gandhi on the Salt Satyagraha, a son of this village on the march that changed India.

  2. 1942

    Quit India

    A. K. Ramankutty of Elappully leads local protests during the Quit India movement. He later served as a social worker and MLA.

  3. 1996

    Sarvodaya Kendram

    Pudussery Sreenivasan founds Sarvodaya Kendram in Ramassery, with Bheeman Guruji, a direct disciple of Gandhi, as its first Chairman.

  4. 2021

    Gandhi Ashram

    The Ashram is founded at Sarvodaya Kendram's Silver Jubilee, in the 75th year of Independence, and dedicated to Thappan Nair and A. K. Ramankutty.

Whom we remember

Two freedom fighters of Elappully.

Ramassery Vadavatte Thappan Nair

Dandi March satyagrahi. He walked with Gandhi in the 1930 Salt Satyagraha, and his name is the reason this village has a Gandhi Ashram.

A. K. Ramankutty

Freedom fighter from Elappully who led local Quit India protests in 1942, then served his people as a social worker and Member of the Legislative Assembly.

Do you have a photograph of them?

We are collecting original photographs, letters and memories of Thappan Nair, A. K. Ramankutty and Elappully's freedom struggle. If your family has any, please get in touch. Every item will be credited to the family that shares it.

The organisation behind the Ashram

Sarvodaya Kendram

Sarvodaya Kendram is a Gandhian organisation founded in 1996 and registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. Guided by the legacy of Bheeman Guruji and led by its founder Director, Pudussery Sreenivasan, it has worked for nearly three decades towards a Sarvodaya society, built on the welfare of all.

What we work on

  • Natural & sustainable farming
  • Farmers' collectives
  • Local self-governance
  • Holistic health care
  • Handloom, khadi & village industries
  • Education for SC & marginalised families
  • Youth & women's leadership
  • Water management
  • Good Food Movement

Nearly three decades of work, including

  • Formation and training of farmers' collectives, and the Krishi Koottam network
  • Grama Panchayat irrigation planning, water budgeting and rainwater harvesting
  • Strengthening Grama Sabhas, Ayal Sabhas and grassroots planning
  • Good-governance action: RTI and social audit
  • Social marketing support for handloom, khadi and village industries
  • Education and empowerment for Scheduled Caste families
  • “Youth against Violence” cycle yatra: 10 young people, Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram, 1,500 km
  • Three and a half years of rehabilitation work in tsunami-affected areas
  • Land, water and forest programme for Adivasi and Dalit communities
  • Gandhi Darshan training, and capacity building for college students with NSS and Unnat Bharat Abhiyan teams
Gandhian network

Part of a national movement.
