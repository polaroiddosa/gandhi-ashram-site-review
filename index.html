Ramassery · Elappully · Palakkad

Swaraj begins in the village.

Gandhi Ashram, Ramassery is a living memorial to Kerala's freedom struggle and a working centre for Gandhian rural transformation, building Elappully into a model Gram Swaraj Panchayat by 2030.

Who we are

A freedom memorial that keeps working.

Modelled on Sevagram Ashram in Wardha, the Ashram was founded in 2021, the Silver Jubilee of Sarvodaya Kendram and the 75th year of India's Independence. It honours two sons of this soil: Dandi March satyagrahi Ramassery Vadavatte Thappan Nair and freedom fighter A. K. Ramankutty.

Behind it stand nearly three decades of grassroots work by Sarvodaya Kendram, in farming, village industries, local self-governance, health and education for the most marginalised families.

All projects

Young Gandhi Fellowship

A one-year social leadership and community service programme for degree, postgraduate and PhD students across Palakkad district. Fellows learn in the field: surveys, documentation, awareness work and action projects alongside the community.

About the Fellowship

Sustainable Farming: Krishi Swaraj

Reducing costs and raising incomes for Elappully's farming families through natural and eco-friendly farming, soil health, shared machinery, value addition and direct market access through farmer collectives.

  • Pilot: 10 Krishi Koottams and 200 farmers
  • Farmer Producer Organisations for direct market linkage
Read more

Ramassery Idli Heritage Enterprise

Only nine families still make the famous Ramassery Idli for a living. The project helps them build a dignified heritage business through hygienic processing, longer shelf life, packaging, branding and heritage food tourism, without compromising the recipe.

Read more

Thenari Weavers Development

Rebuilding livelihoods for traditional handloom and khadi artisan families through design support, better production quality, technology, branding, e-commerce and stronger cooperatives.

Read more

Holistic Health & Wellness

Healthy-lifestyle education, mental wellness and meditation, elderly care and active ageing, and community health awareness across Elappully.

Read more

Antyodaya: SC Community Development

In Gandhi's words, uplifting the last person first. Education, livelihoods, land security, youth and women's leadership, and social dignity for Scheduled Caste families in around 70 settlements (about 2,500 families).

Read more

Gram Swaraj: Living Local Democracy

Training Ayalkoottams, Ayal Sabhas, ward committees and Gram Sabhas across every ward of Elappully Grama Panchayat, with ward-level development plans, monthly review and social accountability.

Train → Empower → Plan → Implement → Review → Improve

Our journey

From the Salt March to Gram Swaraj.

Full history
  1. 1930

    Dandi March

    Ramassery Vadavatte Thappan Nair walks with Gandhi in the Salt Satyagraha.

  2. 1942

    Quit India

    A. K. Ramankutty leads local protests in Elappully; he later served as an MLA.

  3. 1996

    Sarvodaya Kendram

    Founded by Pudussery Sreenivasan, with Bheeman Guruji as its first Chairman.

  4. 2021

    Gandhi Ashram

    Founded at Sarvodaya Kendram's Silver Jubilee, in the 75th year of Independence.
