Volunteer with us

Give your time to Gram Swaraj.

Bring a skill, a few days or a few months. Work alongside Elappully's farmers, artisans and families on the Gandhi Gram projects.

Offer to volunteer
Who can volunteer

There is a place for every skill.

Students

Short placements, internships and field work. For a full year of service, join the Young Gandhi Fellowship.

Professionals

Farmers, doctors, teachers, designers, engineers and researchers who can lend their expertise to a project.

Retired people

Experience and patience: mentoring young people, teaching, or helping run the Ashram's programmes.

Company teams

Employee volunteering alongside a CSR partnership. Team volunteering days to be confirmed.

Ways to help

Six places your time makes a difference.

About the projects

Farming

Work with the Krishi Koottam farmers' groups on natural farming and farmers' livelihoods.

Heritage crafts & food

Help the Ramassery Idli makers and Thenari weavers with branding, product stories and catalogues.

Health

Doctors, nurses and health workers can support the Holistic Health project and awareness work in the wards.

Teaching & mentoring

Support education in the SC settlements under the Antyodaya project, and Nai Talim community learning.

Research & documentation

Household surveys, ward-level studies and oral histories of Elappully's freedom struggle for the Ashram's archive.

Design & communication

Photography, video, writing, design and social media for the Ashram and its projects.

How it works

Four steps from first message to first day.

  1. Write to us with your skills and the time you can give
  2. We call you to find the right project
  3. Visit the Ashram and meet the team
  4. Start work with the project team

Before you come

  • Minimum age to volunteer
  • Shortest stay or time commitment
  • Whether stay and food are available at the Ashram
  • Any costs for the volunteer
