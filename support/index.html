Support the Ashram

Help this heritage keep working.

Every contribution, of any size, helps keep a living memorial of the freedom struggle open, and helps Elappully's families build Gram Swaraj.

Where your support goes

Four places it makes a difference.

The Ashram

Roof and wall repairs, monsoon-proofing and upkeep of the Ashram building.

Gandhi Gram projects

Farming, heritage livelihoods, health, Antyodaya and Gram Swaraj work in Elappully.

Young people

The Young Gandhi Fellowship, study classes and education support for children.

Heritage

Archiving the documents, photographs and oral histories of Elappully's freedom struggle.
