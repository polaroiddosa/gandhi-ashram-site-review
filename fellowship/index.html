Young Gandhi Fellowship

Learn. Serve. Lead.

A one-year social leadership and community service programme for students in Palakkad district, where classroom knowledge meets village life.

How to join
Who can apply

Open to serious students, not only NSS or NCC members.

The main qualification is a willingness to learn, to serve, and to stay committed for a full year.

Open to

  • Degree students
  • Postgraduate students
  • PhD scholars

We look for

  • Interest in community service
  • Leadership potential
  • Commitment to complete the required 120 hours of service
  • Willingness to work in rural communities

Every department has a place: social work, management, media, computer science, agriculture, psychology, commerce, education and engineering.

The one-year journey

Six stages, one year.

  1. Orientation
  2. Swaraj Freedom Course
  3. Project participation
  4. Community action
  5. Documentation portfolio
  6. Final presentation

Certification requires the orientation, the Swaraj course, a minimum of 120 service hours, active project work, documentation, a community action project and a final presentation. The certificate means something because it is backed by real hours and real results.

Your role

Five ways to contribute from day one.

Community Researcher

Surveys, needs assessments and data, including water mapping, farmer interviews and market research.

Field Volunteer

Service, community mobilisation and awareness campaigns across the wards.

Storyteller

Photos, videos, village stories, product stories and catalogues for heritage enterprises.

Project Coordinator

Planning, follow-up and teamwork with ward committees and project teams.

Youth Ambassador

Carrying the message of peace, service and Swaraj to your campus and beyond.

Which project?

Water, farming, heritage, health, education, documentation, leadership or social inclusion: you choose where to begin.

Beyond the classroom

What everyone gains.

Students

Confidence through real responsibility, communication across backgrounds, empathy, leadership and teamwork, and a portfolio of real community impact.

Colleges

Community connection, field learning, research opportunities, social responsibility and recognition for your students.

The community

Youth support, documentation, awareness and volunteer energy, with progress the village can see.

How to join

Applications come through your college.

  1. Your college announces the Fellowship
  2. You apply
  3. Your college nominates you
  4. Interview and selection
  5. Orientation begins
