Planned · Action Research Institute for Gram Swaraj

A campus where research serves the village.

To make Gandhi Gram last, Gandhi Ashram plans a dedicated institute: ARIGS, an eco-campus for Gandhian action research, training and practice.

Why an institute

Rigour, continuity, and a place to learn.

ARIGS will give the Gram Swaraj work long-term sustainability, academic rigour and grassroots reach. It will be a laboratory where rural development models are tested and grown, and a resource centre that others can learn from.

  • Incubate grassroots solutions: test and scale rural development models in the field
  • Empower the community: train local youth as self-reliant action researchers, the Gram Sevaks
  • Knowledge hub: a national resource centre for Gandhian action research, with workshops, exposure visits and policy dialogues
Campus master plan

Aparigraha in architecture.

Only 30–40% of the land will be built on. The rest stays green: organic farms, open-air learning spaces and tree cover, as a working prototype for the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Academic & Administration

Administration, digital archives, research labs, conference rooms and a library on Gandhian economics.

Gram Swaraj Innovation Hub

A Ramassery Idli heritage lab, a khadi and handloom design studio, and an agri-innovation centre.

Eco-Agriculture

Organic demonstration plots, a seed bank, a soil lab and composting units.

Community Training

A 200-seat open-air bamboo amphitheatre and seminar rooms for Gram Sabha dialogue.

Green Residency

Mud-and-bamboo cottages for visiting scholars, and the Annapoorna dining hall.

Built with the earth

Zero-carbon, low-rise, self-reliant.

  • Low, horizontal buildings, no higher than ground plus one floor
  • Stabilised mud blocks, bamboo, unglazed terracotta and stone, with no carbon-heavy concrete
  • Solar power for the whole campus
  • Rainwater-harvesting reservoirs and decentralised greywater treatment
People & governance

Led by practice, guided by expertise.

Chief Mentor

A Gandhian practitioner with long experience in non-violent social action and constructive work, who lives the message as well as teaching it.

Action research team

Experts in agrarian economy, rural enterprise and heritage branding, social inclusion and Dalit empowerment, and community education, with field coordinators in every ward.

Two-tier governance

A Governing Council for policy and oversight, and an Academic Advisory Panel of researchers, practitioners and community champions.
